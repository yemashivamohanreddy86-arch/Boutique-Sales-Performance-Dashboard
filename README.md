
# Boutique Sales & Performance Dashboard

## Project Overview
This project presents an interactive Boutique Sales & Performance Dashboard developed using Microsoft Power BI.

The dashboard helps boutique businesses monitor sales, profitability, product performance, city-wise revenue and customer payment preferences.

**Note:** This project uses fictional demonstration data for portfolio purposes.

## Business Objectives
- Monitor overall sales and profit.
- Understand product category performance.
- Compare sales across different cities.
- Analyze customer payment preferences.
- Track monthly sales trends.
- Enable interactive business analysis.

## Dataset
- 600 fictional sales transactions
- 14 data columns
- 12 product variations
- 6 product categories
- 6 cities
- 5 payment methods
- 3 sales channels

## Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)
1. Total Sales
2. Total Profit
3. Total Orders
4. Total Products
5. Average Order Value

## Dashboard Visualizations
- Monthly Sales Trend
- Sales by Product Category
- Sales by City
- Sales by Payment Method

## Interactive Filters
- City
- Product Category
- Sales Channel

## Tools & Technologies
- Microsoft Excel
- Microsoft Power BI
- Power Query
- DAX (Data Analysis Expressions)

## Business Value
This dashboard demonstrates how retail businesses can use data visualization and analytics to understand sales performance, identify profitable product categories and make informed business decisions.
